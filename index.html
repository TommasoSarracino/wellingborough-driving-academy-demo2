Common questions

How long is a lesson?

A full hour, with only one pupil in the car.

Will my price go up during my course?

No. Once you start a course, your fees stay the same.

Do you offer courses for people who have passed?

Yes: Refresher, Motorway, Advanced and Pass Plus.

Can I learn in my own car?

Yes, and own-car lessons are discounted.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 6am to 8pm. We’re closed on Saturday and Sunday. For lessons in the evenings or at weekends, please call to ask.

Is theory help really free?

Yes. Free theory tuition is available to all, with no obligation.

Do you book my test?

Yes, we can apply for your test at no extra cost. You pay only the official DVSA fee.

What if I have a concern?

Speak to your instructor first. If it isn’t resolved, tell the proprietor. If it’s still not settled, it can be studied by instructorconduct@dvsa.gov.uk.
